    Electrical design notes
    • USB powers only the ESP32; its 5 V header is unused.
    • External 5 V enters through ACS712 IP+ / IP−.
    • HC-SR04 ECHO is divided by 2.2 kΩ / 3.3 kΩ.
    • Servo power is independent with 470 µF + 100 nF decoupling.
